# 🏥 My Favorite Series: *Doctor Stranger*

## 🌟 Overview
**Doctor Stranger** (닥터 이방인) is a 2014 South Korean medical drama starring *Lee Jong-suk* as Park Hoon, a gifted thoracic surgeon trained in North Korea. After escaping to South Korea, he struggles to fit in while saving lives and searching for his lost love.

> “I will save the patient, no matter what.” – Park Hoon

---

### 👨‍⚕️ Key Characters
1. **Park Hoon** – Genius surgeon with a painful past  
2. **Song Jae-hee / Han Seung-hee** – Park Hoon’s love interest  
3. **Han Jae-joon** – Ambitious doctor with a hidden motive  
4. **Oh Soo-hyun** – Warm-hearted surgeon and daughter of the hospital director
